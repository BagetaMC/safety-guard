# Safety-Guard – Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** October 31, 2026
**Contact:** bageta.private@gmail.com

This Privacy Policy explains what data the Discord bot **Safety-Guard** ("the Bot", "we") collects, why, and how it is handled. By adding the Bot to a server or interacting with it, you agree to this policy.

## 1. What the Bot is

Safety-Guard is a security and moderation bot for Discord. It provides CAPTCHA verification, a ticket system, anti-spam, anti-raid, anti-nuke, anti-link, anti-mention and anti-bot protection, moderation commands (warn, kick, ban), security logs and a server lockdown function.

## 2. Data we store

The Bot stores only the data needed to work. This data is saved in files on the Bot's hosting server:

- **Server (guild) settings:** server ID, IDs of configured channels and roles (for example the ticket category, support roles, log channel, verification role), panel and embed texts and colors, and security settings (for example anti-link mode, mention limit, anti-bot allowed user IDs).
- **Warnings:** server ID, user ID of the warned member, the reason given by the moderator, and the time of the warning.
- **Tickets:** the IDs of the ticket channel and of the user who opened it, so that the Bot can manage and close the ticket.
- **CAPTCHA data:** user ID, server ID and the number and time of failed verification attempts (to apply a cooldown).
- **Lockdown data:** the previous permission state of channels, so that it can be restored after a lockdown ends.

The Bot does not store your name, email address, IP address or any payment information.

## 3. Data we process but do not store

- **Message content.** To provide anti-spam, anti-link and anti-mention protection, the Bot reads messages sent in servers where it is present. Message content is evaluated in memory and is **not saved** by the Bot.
- **Member and audit information.** The Bot reads member join/leave events, role changes, bans, timeouts, channel and role changes and the Discord audit log in order to provide protection and logging.

## 4. Logs and transcripts on your server

If a server administrator enables the log function, the Bot posts information (such as deleted or edited message content, joins and leaves, bans, timeouts, role and channel changes) **into the log channel chosen by that server**. When a ticket is closed, a text transcript of the ticket conversation is posted to the configured log channel. This data is stored by Discord in that server, is controlled by the server's administrators, and is not sent anywhere else by the Bot.

Server administrators are responsible for informing their members that logging is enabled.

## 5. Why we use the data

We use data only to operate the Bot's features: moderation, security, verification, support tickets and logging. We do not use data for advertising or profiling.

## 6. Sharing of data

We do **not sell, rent or share** your data with third parties. Data is processed by Discord Inc. (as the platform the Bot runs on) and stored on the hosting provider that runs the Bot, solely to keep the Bot working. We may disclose data if required by law.

## 7. Retention and deletion

Data is kept as long as the Bot is used on the server. When the Bot is removed from a server, its data is not deleted automatically. You can request deletion at any time by emailing **bageta.private@gmail.com** with your server ID or user ID. We will delete the data within 30 days.

## 8. Your rights (including GDPR)

If you are in the European Economic Area or the UK, you have the right to access the data we hold about you, to have it corrected or deleted, and to object to its processing. To exercise these rights, contact **bageta.private@gmail.com**.

## 9. Children

The Bot is intended for users who meet Discord's minimum age requirement (13 or older, or higher where local law requires). We do not knowingly collect data from children under that age.

## 10. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect stored data, but no system is completely secure and we cannot guarantee absolute security.
